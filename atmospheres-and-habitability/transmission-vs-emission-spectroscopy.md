# Transmission vs emission spectroscopy

### Transmission (during transit)

* Probes the terminator region.
* Sensitive to scale height.
* Strongly affected by clouds and hazes.

### Emission (secondary eclipse)

* Probes the dayside.
* Sensitive to temperature profile.
* Can constrain energy balance with phase curves.

### When they disagree

That’s common.

* Different regions (terminator vs dayside).
* Dynamics redistribute heat and chemistry.
* Stellar contamination biases transit spectra.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A flat transmission spectrum is not “no atmosphere” by default. It can be high clouds or high mean molecular weight.
{% endhint %}
