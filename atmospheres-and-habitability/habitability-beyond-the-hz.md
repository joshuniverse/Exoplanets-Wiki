# Habitability beyond the HZ

The habitable zone is one axis. Habitability is multi-parameter.

### Key factors

* **Stellar activity**: flares, UV, particle events.
* **Tidal locking**: affects circulation and climate.
* **Magnetic environment**: influences escape and radiation.
* **Atmosphere retention**: depends on gravity and irradiation.

### M dwarf specifics

* Close-in HZ means stronger tidal and activity effects.
* Stellar contamination complicates spectroscopy.

{% hint style="info" %}
A good habitability claim always specifies the assumed atmosphere.
{% endhint %}
