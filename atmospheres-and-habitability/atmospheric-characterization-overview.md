# Atmospheric characterization (overview)

Atmospheres are measured indirectly. You observe photons that interacted with gas.

### Main observation types

* **Transmission spectroscopy**: starlight filtered through the limb.
* **Emission / eclipse spectroscopy**: planet’s dayside light.
* **Phase curves**: brightness and spectrum vs orbital phase.
* **Direct-imaging spectra**: thermal and reflected light for wide planets.

### What you can infer

* Molecular absorption features.
* Temperature structure (limited).
* Clouds/hazes (often dominant).

### What dominates the error budget

* Stellar contamination.
* Instrument systematics.
* Model assumptions.
