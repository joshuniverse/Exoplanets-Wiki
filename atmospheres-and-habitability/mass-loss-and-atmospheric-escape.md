# Mass loss and atmospheric escape

Close-in planets lose atmospheres. This shapes the observed radius distribution.

### Main mechanisms

* **Photoevaporation**: XUV heating drives escape.
* **Core-powered mass loss**: cooling luminosity can unbind gas.

### Observational signposts

* The **radius valley** between super-Earths and sub-Neptunes.
* Escaping hydrogen seen in Lyman-$$\alpha$$ and helium 1083 nm.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Escape observations are star-dependent. Interstellar absorption and stellar variability can dominate.
{% endhint %}
