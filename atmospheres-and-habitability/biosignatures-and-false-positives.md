# Biosignatures and false positives

A **biosignature** is a pattern that is hard to explain without biology. In practice, it’s usually a _hypothesis test_.

### Commonly discussed candidate gases

* O$\_2$ / O$\_3$
* CH$\_4$
* CO$\_2$
* N$\_2$O (more speculative observationally)

### Why false positives are a big deal

Abiotic processes can mimic “life-like” gases. Examples include photochemistry and water loss.

### What “context” usually means

* Host star UV spectrum and activity.
* Planet mass/radius (retention and geology).
* Presence of H$\_2$O, CO, CO$\_2$.
* Surface pressure constraints.

{% hint style="warning" %}
No single molecule is a biosignature in isolation. Treat biosignatures as _sets_ + context.
{% endhint %}
