# Directly imaged planets (benchmarks)

Direct imaging targets are usually:

* Young (still warm).
* Wide orbit.
* Around nearby stars.

### Benchmark systems

* **HR 8799 b/c/d/e**: multi-planet directly imaged system.
* **Beta Pictoris b**: planet in a famous debris disk system.
* **51 Eridani b**: methane-bearing young giant.

### What you actually get

* Photometry and spectra.
* Orbital motion over years.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Masses come from cooling models + age estimates. Treat “mass” as model-dependent unless dynamically measured.
{% endhint %}
