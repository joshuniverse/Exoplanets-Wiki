# Potentially habitable (targets people argue about)

“Potentially habitable” is usually shorthand for **size + irradiation**. It is not a life claim.

### Commonly discussed targets

* **Proxima Centauri b**: nearest known planet candidate around the nearest star.
* **TRAPPIST-1 e/f/g**: compact system of small planets around an ultracool dwarf.
* **LHS 1140 b**: dense, temperate super-Earth around an M dwarf.
* **Kepler-186 f**: one of the first Earth-size planets in a star’s HZ (Kepler-era).

### What makes these hard

* M dwarfs flare.
* Stellar contamination biases spectra.
* “HZ” depends on atmosphere assumptions.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Don’t quote “habitable” without stating the criterion. Use insolation or $$T_{eq}$$ if you can.
{% endhint %}
