# Hot Jupiters (benchmark systems)

Hot Jupiters are oversized and easy to detect. They were the first exoplanets found in large numbers.

### Benchmark examples

* **51 Peg b**: the original hot Jupiter archetype.
* **HD 209458 b**: first transiting hot Jupiter with deep atmospheric work.
* **HD 189733 b**: bright host star, lots of atmospheric studies.
* **WASP-12 b**: extreme irradiation and orbital decay discussions.
* **KELT-9 b**: ultra-hot giant on the edge of “planet vs star-like” physics.

### Why they matter

* Big atmospheric scale heights.
* Strong phase-curve signals.
* Great for instrument shakedown.

{% hint style="info" %}
Many hot Jupiters have inflated radii. Density can be surprisingly low.
{% endhint %}
