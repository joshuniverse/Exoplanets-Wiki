# Extreme worlds and record holders

Outliers are useful. They stress-test models and reveal selection effects.

### Common categories

* **Ultra-short-period (USP)**: periods under \~1 day.
* **Lava worlds**: very hot rocky planets with possible magma oceans.
* **Puffy planets**: very low-density sub-Saturns.
* **Eccentric giants**: extreme seasons and tidal evolution.

### Often-cited examples

* **55 Cancri e**: bright host, high-temperature rocky planet archetype.
* **WASP-107 b**: very low density, strong mass-loss discussions.
* **HD 80606 b**: famously eccentric giant.

{% hint style="info" %}
“Record holder” status changes fast. Always check the current catalog.
{% endhint %}
