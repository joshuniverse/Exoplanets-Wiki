# Multi-planet systems and resonances

Multi-planet systems are where dynamics becomes observable.

### Why they matter

* TTVs can measure masses.
* Resonances constrain migration and formation.
* Architectures inform demographics.

### Commonly cited systems

* **TRAPPIST-1**: compact chain of small planets.
* **Kepler-11**: classic compact multi with low densities.
* **Kepler-36**: two planets with very different densities, close together.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Published architectures can change as new planets are found. Treat them as snapshots.
{% endhint %}
