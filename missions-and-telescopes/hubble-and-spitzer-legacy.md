# Hubble and Spitzer legacy

### Hubble (HST)

* Built much of the transmission spectroscopy literature.
* Strong for optical/near-IR features.

### Spitzer

* Key for thermal emission and phase curves.
* Anchored many temperature and circulation results.

### Why the legacy data is still used

* Long time baseline.
* Cross-instrument consistency checks.
* Re-analysis with better methods.

{% hint style="info" %}
JWST doesn’t replace HST for everything. Optical coverage and historical baselines still matter.
{% endhint %}
