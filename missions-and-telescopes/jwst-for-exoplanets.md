# JWST for exoplanets

JWST enables high-S/N atmospheric spectroscopy.

### Common observing modes

* **Transmission**: during transit.
* **Emission**: secondary eclipse.
* **Phase curves**: brightness vs orbital phase.

### What JWST is good at

* Molecular bands in the near-IR and mid-IR.
* Small planets around small stars (best targets).

### Common pitfalls

* Stellar heterogeneity biases spectra.
* Instrument systematics need careful modeling.
