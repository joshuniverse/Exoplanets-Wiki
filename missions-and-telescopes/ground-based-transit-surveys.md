# Ground-based transit surveys

Ground-based transit surveys found many hot Jupiters. They also do critical follow-up today.

### Common surveys and networks

* **WASP**
* **HATNet / HATSouth**
* **NGTS**
* **KELT**
* **TRAPPIST**

### What they’re good at

* Bright targets.
* Short periods.
* Rapid follow-up.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Ground-based photometry is atmosphere-limited. Systematics and red noise dominate.
{% endhint %}
