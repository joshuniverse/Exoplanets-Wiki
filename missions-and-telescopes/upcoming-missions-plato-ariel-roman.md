# Upcoming missions: PLATO, ARIEL, Roman

### PLATO (ESA)

* Transits + asteroseismology.
* Focus on bright stars and precise stellar parameters.

### ARIEL (ESA)

* Atmospheric survey mission.
* Goal: population-level composition trends.

### Roman (NASA)

* Microlensing survey for cold-planet demographics.
* Coronagraph technology development (program-dependent).
