# TESS

### What TESS is optimized for

* Bright stars.
* Short-to-moderate periods.
* Follow-up-friendly targets.

### Why TESS dominates current follow-up

* Bright hosts make RV and spectroscopy feasible.
* Continuous viewing zones support long baselines.

### Community workflow

* Candidate list: TOIs.
* Follow-up coordination: ExoFOP-TESS.

### Data access

* MAST for light curves and target pixel files.

{% hint style="warning" %}
TESS pixels are large. Blends are common. High-res imaging is routine follow-up.
{% endhint %}
