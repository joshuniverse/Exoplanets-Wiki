# Kepler and K2

### What Kepler did

* Stared at one field for years.
* Pushed transit detection into small-planet territory.

### What K2 changed

* Observed many fields along the ecliptic.
* Shorter baselines, broader target types.

### Why it still matters

* Occurrence rates and planet demographics.
* Long baselines for multi-year periods.
* Benchmark light curves for methodology.

### Data access

* Use **MAST** for light curves and pixel data.
* Many reprocessed light curves exist.
