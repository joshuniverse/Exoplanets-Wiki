# High-contrast imaging instruments

Direct imaging is usually done with adaptive optics and coronagraphy.

### Common instruments

* **SPHERE** (VLT)
* **GPI** (Gemini)
* **SCExAO** (Subaru)

### What to look for in results

* Contrast curves.
* Astrometric calibration.
* Common proper motion checks.

{% hint style="info" %}
A “candidate companion” needs multi-epoch confirmation. Background objects are common.
{% endhint %}
