# Extremely Large Telescopes (ELT/TMT/GMT)

ELTs change exoplanets via:

* Higher spatial resolution.
* More photons for high-dispersion spectroscopy.
* Better contrasts for imaging.

### Expected science areas

* Atmospheric composition for smaller planets.
* High-resolution Doppler spectroscopy of atmospheres.
* Imaging of cooler and lower-mass giants.

{% hint style="info" %}
ELT-era work often combines AO + high-dispersion spectroscopy. It’s a different regime than broadband imaging.
{% endhint %}
