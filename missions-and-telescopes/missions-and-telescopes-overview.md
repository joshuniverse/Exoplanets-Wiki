# Missions & telescopes (overview)

Exoplanet work is usually a pipeline:

* Wide-field **survey** finds candidates.
* **Follow-up** confirms and measures masses.
* **Characterization** gets atmospheres and dynamics.

### Typical roles

* Surveys: Kepler/K2, TESS, ground-based transit surveys.
* Mass follow-up: precision RV spectrographs.
* Atmospheres: HST and JWST, plus large ground-based telescopes.
* Demographics: microlensing surveys, Gaia astrometry.

### Archives you’ll touch

* MAST for Kepler/TESS/HST/JWST time series.
* Mission archives for raw and calibrated products.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you’re tracing a result, find the archive product ID. That’s the reproducibility anchor.
{% endhint %}
