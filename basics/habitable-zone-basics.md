# Habitable zone (basics)

The **habitable zone (HZ)** is the range of distances where a rocky planet _could_ sustain liquid water.

That’s a climate statement. Not a biosignature.

### The quick mental model

* Too close: runaway greenhouse.
* Too far: global glaciation.

### Why the HZ is not a promise

* You still need an atmosphere.
* You still need the right volatile inventory.
* Clouds and greenhouse gases shift the boundaries.

### Star type matters

* M dwarfs: close-in HZ, but stronger flares and tidal effects.
* Sun-like stars: wider HZ, longer periods.

{% hint style="info" %}
People often quote HZ as insolation ($$S$$) rather than distance ($$a$$).
{% endhint %}
