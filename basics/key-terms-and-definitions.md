# Key terms & definitions

### Object status

* **Candidate**: not fully vetted. Often labeled as _KOI_, _TOI_, or _planet candidate_.
* **Validated**: statistically shown to be planetary. No mass required.
* **Confirmed**: typically has a mass measurement or multiple independent confirmations.
* **False positive**: eclipsing binary, background blend, instrumental artifact, or stellar activity.

### Core planet properties

* **Radius (**$$R_p$$**)**: usually from transits.
* **Mass (**$$M_p$$**)**: usually from RVs or TTVs.
* **Density**: derived from mass + radius.
* **Period (**$$P$$**)** and **semi-major axis (**$$a$$**)**: orbit size and timescale.
* **Eccentricity (**$$e$$**)**: orbit shape.

### Irradiation and temperature

* **Insolation (**$$S$$**)**: stellar energy received vs Earth.
* **Equilibrium temperature (**$$T_{eq}$$**)**: rough temperature from energy balance.
* **Albedo**: how reflective the planet is.

{% hint style="warning" %}
$$T_{eq}$$ is not a surface temperature. It ignores greenhouse effects.
{% endhint %}

### Host star properties that matter

* **Stellar radius and mass**: set planet radius and orbit scale.
* **Effective temperature**: affects irradiation.
* **Metallicity**: correlates with giant planet occurrence.
* **Activity / rotation**: can mimic RV signals.

<details>

<summary>Common acronyms</summary>

* **RV**: radial velocity.
* **TTV**: transit timing variations.
* **S/N**: signal-to-noise ratio.
* **SFP**: small false positive (used loosely).

</details>
