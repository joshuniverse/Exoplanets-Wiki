# Glossary (A–Z)

### A

* **Albedo**: fraction of light reflected.
* **Astrometry**: measuring sky-position wobbles.

### C

* **Candidate**: signal consistent with a planet.
* **Confirmation**: dynamical or multi-method evidence.

### D

* **Doppler / RV**: line-of-sight velocity method.

### E

* **Equilibrium temperature (**$$T_{eq}$$**)**: simple energy-balance temperature.
* **Eccentricity (**$$e$$**)**: orbit shape.

### H

* **Habitable zone (HZ)**: where liquid water could be possible.
* **Hot Jupiter**: short-period gas giant.

### I

* **Insolation (**$$S$$**)**: stellar flux received relative to Earth.

### M

* **Microlensing**: temporary brightening from gravitational lensing.

### P

* **Phase curve**: brightness change with orbital phase.
* **Puffy planet**: very low-density sub-Saturn.

### R

* **Radius valley**: gap in the radius distribution near \~1.5–2 $$R_\oplus$$.
* **Retrieval**: Bayesian inference of atmospheric parameters from spectra.

### T

* **Transit**: planet crosses star.
* **TTV**: transit timing variations.

### V

* **Validation**: statistical demonstration that the planet scenario dominates.
