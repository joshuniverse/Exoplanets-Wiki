# Measuring exoplanet properties

### Radius

* **Transits** measure a depth $$\Delta F / F$$.
* Planet radius depends on stellar radius.

Rule of thumb:

* $$\text{depth} \approx (R_p/R_\star)^2$$

### Mass

Common mass sources:

* **Radial velocity (RV)**: gives $$M_p \sin i$$.
* **TTVs**: uses gravitational interactions in multi-planet systems.
* **Astrometry**: measures the star’s sky-plane wobble.

### Density and composition

Density is simple algebra. Interpretation is not.

* Rocky vs volatile-rich depends on interior models.
* Atmospheres can inflate radii.

### Atmospheres

You’ll see:

* **Transmission spectra** (during transit).
* **Emission spectra** (secondary eclipse / phase curves).
* **Direct-imaging spectra** (for wide, young giants).

{% hint style="warning" %}
Most parameters are dominated by **stellar** uncertainties. Get the star wrong and the planet follows.
{% endhint %}
