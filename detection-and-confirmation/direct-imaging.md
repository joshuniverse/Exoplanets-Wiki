# Direct imaging

**Direct imaging** separates a planet’s light from its star. It’s hard because stars are bright.

### What you measure

* Planet brightness in one or more bands.
* Colors and spectra (if you get them).
* Projected separation on the sky.

### What imaging tends to find

* Young, self-luminous gas giants.
* Wide orbits (tens of AU).

### Key constraints

* Contrast limit (how faint you can go).
* Inner working angle (how close to the star you can see).

### Common interpretation pitfalls

* Brightness $$\rightarrow$$ mass depends on cooling models.
* Age of the system matters a lot.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A direct image is not a mass measurement. It’s usually a luminosity measurement.
{% endhint %}
