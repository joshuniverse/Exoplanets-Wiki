# Astrometry

**Astrometry** measures a star’s tiny motion on the sky. A planet pulls the star around the barycenter.

### What astrometry gives you

* Orbital inclination.
* True planet mass (not $$M\sin i$$).
* Long-period sensitivity (in principle).

### What makes it hard

* You need micro-arcsecond precision.
* Systematics dominate.

### Where it shows up today

* Gaia is the big driver.
* Astrometry is often combined with RVs.

{% hint style="info" %}
Astrometry + RV breaks the $$\sin i$$ degeneracy. That’s how you catch face-on “planets” that are really stars.
{% endhint %}
