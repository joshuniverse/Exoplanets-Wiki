# Radial velocity (Doppler)

**Radial velocity (RV)** measures the star’s line-of-sight speed. A planet makes the star wobble.

### What RV measures

* Velocity semi-amplitude $$K$$.
* Minimum mass $$M_p \sin i$$.
* Orbital period and eccentricity.

### Strengths

* Works without transits.
* Gives mass constraints.
* Can detect non-transiting planets.

### Limits and gotchas

* Stellar activity can mimic planets.
* Multi-planet systems can alias.
* Instrument drift and tellurics matter.

### Common checks in good RV papers

* Activity indicators (e.g., Ca II H\&K).
* Window function / alias analysis.
* Independent instruments.

{% hint style="info" %}
Combine RV + transit and you get density. That’s where composition talk starts.
{% endhint %}
